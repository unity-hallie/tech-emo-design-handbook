Technical Emotion Design

Introduction

This is a handbook for designing things that are hard on purpose. Before you read it, you are going to try one.

Someone wrote instructions for assembling a small picture of five shapes. The instructions did not quite work. You will see the picture and the instructions side by side.

Your job is to revise the instructions so they actually work. Edit them in place. Take as long as you want.

When you are ready — whenever you are ready — close the window. The picture will go away.

Your writing will not.

If you come back later and open the window again, you will get a different picture and different instructions. The pair you saw the first time is gone. What you wrote is how it stays.
