#Words Matter

What is a rose? A rose by any other word would smell as sweet. And that's all well and good, but not when describing instructions to humans or ai or other language reading agents.
An AI can't smell, and how can you tell someone what a rose is without using the word 'rose'? 

In AI, tokens are chunking and chunking is attention. The more you can get across with a few tokens,and the more meaning you can put in the individual words and their positioning, the more able you will be to write prompts, instructions, and conversations that do what you want them to do.
